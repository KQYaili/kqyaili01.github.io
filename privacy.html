Privacy Policy

SynScale is a personal research utility used by its owner.

Google User Data

Google Drive access is used for file synchronization through rclone. Data is accessed only as needed for the owner's requested operations.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's locally managed rclone configuration. Files are synchronized to storage locations controlled by the owner.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. No public service is provided to third-party users.

Revoking Access

Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
